From application to certificate

Four stages. You'll know where you stand at the end of each one.

01

Apply and talk to an advisor

A short form, then a conversation about whether the track you picked is the right one. We'll tell you if it isn't.

02

Pre-work, four weeks, online

You arrive on campus already fluent in the basics, so the two weeks abroad are spent on the hard part.

03

Two weeks on campus

Faculty sessions, site visits, and a project you present to practitioners on the final day.

04

Certificate and mentoring

Your certificate from the host institution, your project written up for your portfolio, and continued access to the cohort.
